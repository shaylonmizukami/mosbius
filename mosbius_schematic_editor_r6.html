Model & caveats
The die is 65 nm CMOS thick-oxide devices at 2.5 V. The TSMC 0.25 µm card shipped with the LTspice library — and used here — is a stand-in Columbia reports tracks measurement closely. Compact MOSFET model: EKV-style soft threshold (so subthreshold slope and the gm/ID ceiling are right), velocity saturation, channel-length modulation, built from vth0, tox, u0 and the real device geometry. Transient uses trapezoidal integration with adaptive stepping.

Not modelled: the 15 Ω crossbar switches, the ESD series resistors, and pad / bus capacitance — the last is not documented anywhere upstream. Expect simulated switching to come out much faster than the bench: the reference 3-stage ring measures 14–18 MHz on silicon and simulates here in the GHz, because only intrinsic device capacitance is present. Add explicit load capacitors to close the gap. Shapes, ratios and trends are trustworthy; absolute speed is not.
